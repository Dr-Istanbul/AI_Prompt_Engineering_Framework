# 🛠️ Setup Guide: Launch Your AI Agency in 48 Hours

## Phase 1: Tool Stack Setup (Day 1)

### Essential AI Tools:
1. **Lovable.design** - Website redesign platform
   - Account creation: 5 minutes
   - Basic training: 30 minutes
   - Advanced features: 1 hour

2. **LLM Access** (ChatGPT Plus/Claude Pro)
   - Account setup: 10 minutes
   - Prompt testing: 1 hour

3. **Communication Tools**
   - Professional email: yourname@youragency.com
   - Calendly for scheduling: 15 minutes
   - Slack/Discord for client comms: 10 minutes

### Phase 2: Portfolio Development (Day 2)
1. **Select 3-5 practice websites** (local businesses, friends)
2. **Create redesign concepts** using our prompt templates
3. **Build portfolio page** with before/after examples
4. **Develop pricing packages** and service descriptions
