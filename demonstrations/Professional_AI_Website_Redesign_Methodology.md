🏗️ The Professional Redesign Framework
Phase 1: Discovery & Analysis Prompt
prompt
ACT as a UX Research Specialist. Analyze [WEBSITE] and identify:

1. **Usability Issues**: List top 5 navigation and flow problems
2. **Conversion Barriers**: Identify 3 primary obstacles to desired actions
3. **Technical Deficiencies**: Page speed, mobile responsiveness, SEO issues
4. **Competitive Gap**: How the site compares to industry leaders

Present findings in a structured report with specific, actionable recommendations.
Phase 2: Strategic Direction Prompt
prompt
ACT as a Senior Digital Strategist. Based on the analysis, develop a redesign strategy that:

- Incorporates industry best practices: [INDUSTRY STANDARDS]
- Includes measurable success criteria: [KPIs]

Provide a phased implementation plan with timeline and resource requirements.

prompt
