# Case Study: Insurance Website Redesign

## Client: Local Insurance Agency
**Original Website Issues:**
- Design from 2008 (15+ years outdated)
- 40% mobile bounce rate
- 5+ second loading time
- Weak lead capture forms

## AI Redesign Process:
1. **Analysis**: Identified key usability issues
2. **Prompt Used**: "Redesign this insurance website focusing on trust signals and mobile optimization"
3. **Tools**: Lovable.design + ChatGPT for content refinement
4. **Timeline**: 2 hours total

## Results:
- **Design**: Modern, trustworthy aesthetic
- **Performance**: 2-second loading time
- **Mobile**: 95%+ score on Google PageSpeed
- **Conversions**: 300% increase in lead form submissions

## Client Feedback:
"The new website looks incredible. We're already seeing more qualified leads coming through in the first week."
